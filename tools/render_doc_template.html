The Museum

A first person puzzle game about being locked inside an alien recreation of a human museum. The halls are almost right. The artifacts are not. This page holds the engine decision and the shape of the code, then the world itself: five civilizations, a way of counting in sixes, and the animals they were dug up beside.

Engine Godot 4.7.1 Forward+, C# Proven here 12 shadowed lights in fog, software Vulkan Input Mouse, keyboard, gamepad World Invented, in the manner of Riven
